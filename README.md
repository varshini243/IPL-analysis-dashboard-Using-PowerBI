# 🏏 **IPL-ANALYSIS-DASHBOARD**

---

## 📌 **Project Overview**

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that analyzes the performance of teams and players in the **Indian Premier League (IPL)**. It helps users explore match trends, top performers, and team statistics visually and easily.

---

## 📊 **Dataset Details**

The project uses two datasets:

### **1️⃣ matches.csv**

Contains match-level information:

* Match ID
* Season
* City & Venue
* Team1 & Team2
* Toss Winner & Toss Decision
* Match Winner
* Result Type
* Player of the Match
* Umpires

### **2️⃣ deliveries.csv**

Contains ball-by-ball match details:

* Match ID
* Over & Ball
* Batting Team / Bowling Team
* Batsman & Bowler
* Runs Scored
* Extras
* Wickets
* Dismissal Details

---

## 📐 **Key Metrics Used**

* Total Runs
* Total Wickets
* Highest Team Score
* Best Batsmen
* Best Bowlers
* Toss Win Impact
* Win Percentage of Teams
* Season-wise Match Trends

---

## 📺 **Dashboard Features**

* Interactive filters for **Season**, **Team**, and **Venue**
* Batsman performance charts
* Bowler performance charts
* Team-wise win rates
* Toss decision analysis
* Match trend line charts
* Top performers summary cards

---

## 💡 **Insights Generated**

* Shows the **best-performing teams** across seasons
* Highlights **top batsmen and bowlers**
* Explains how **toss and venue influence match results**
* Reveals **seasonal performance patterns**
* Helps compare teams and players visually
